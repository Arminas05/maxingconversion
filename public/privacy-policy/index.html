Privacy Policy

Effective date:

This policy explains what information is collected on this site, why, and how it's used. If anything here is unclear, contact and ask.

Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to ("we," "us," "our") and to all pages at this domain, including the sales page, the strategy call booking page, and the pages that follow a booking. By submitting the qualifier form or booking a call, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

When you fill out the qualifier form on this site, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your answers to the qualifying questions (product stage, audience size, monthly revenue range, and timeline)
  • Which page you submitted the form from

When you book a call, our scheduling provider (Calendly or similar) separately collects your chosen appointment time and any information you provide directly to that service. Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policy, not this one.

Information collected automatically

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. As with any website, Cloudflare processes standard technical request data — including your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request — in order to serve the site and protect it from abuse. We do not use this data to identify individual visitors. Cloudflare's privacy practices are described at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Fonts on this site are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when you load a page. See policies.google.com/privacy.

How we use this information

  • To confirm and prepare for your strategy call
  • To send you a reminder by email or text before the call
  • To follow up if you don't attend a booked call
  • To understand, in aggregate, what kinds of businesses use this page — we do not sell or rent this information to third parties

We do not use your phone number for marketing calls or texts unrelated to a call you've booked, and we do not add it to any list without your separate, explicit consent.

How this information is processed

Form submissions on this site are processed through Formspree, a third-party form-handling service, and then reviewed by us via email. Formspree's handling of data in transit is governed by their own privacy policy, available at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

Call scheduling is processed through Calendly (or whichever scheduling tool is in use). Their privacy policy is available at calendly.com/privacy.

Data retention

We retain qualifier submissions and call records for as long as reasonably necessary to service the relationship — typically no longer than 24 months from your last contact with us, unless you ask us to delete it sooner or the law requires us to keep it longer.
