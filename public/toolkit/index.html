How it works, and why it isn't just "ask Claude to write a sales page"

You can already ask any AI to write you a sales page. You'll get something that reads fine and sells nothing — a polite essay about your product, in the shape of a landing page.

The reason is structure. A page that converts isn't good writing arranged nicely; it's a specific sequence, and each section has a job. Call out who should be reading. Name the pain before you name the product. Explain the mechanism enough to be credible but not enough to be a manual. Address the scepticism out loud instead of hoping it goes away. Put the first call to action after you've earned it, not before.

The skills encode that sequence. They ask you the questions the sequence needs answers to, in the order it needs them, and they refuse to skip the sections people usually skip — which are, reliably, the ones doing the selling.

That's the difference between a prompt and a skill. A prompt gives you one answer. A skill runs a process.

What most "write your sales page" advice gets wrong

  • Leading with your story, because you were told stories sell — before the reader has any reason to care who you are.
  • Listing features and modules, as if the reader is comparing spec sheets rather than deciding whether you understand their problem.
  • Opening with the price, or hiding it so thoroughly the page feels evasive.
  • Writing "unlock", "transform", "elevate" in place of saying the actual thing that happens.
  • Putting the only call to action at the very bottom, so the person who was already sold has to scroll past everything to buy.
  • Treating scepticism as something to avoid mentioning, which leaves the reader arguing with you in their head, alone, and winning.

What the structure does instead

  • Names who should stop reading, in the first few lines — which makes everyone who keeps reading more likely to buy.
  • States the pain in the reader's own words before introducing the product at all.
  • Raises the obvious objection out loud, then answers it, so it stops running in the background.
  • Places a call to action the moment the case is made, and again for the people who needed longer.
