Cookie Policy

Effective date:

As it stands, this site sets no analytics or advertising cookies of its own. That changes the moment you add a pixel — this page tells you what has to be updated when you do.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. They can be used to keep a site working correctly, to measure how it is used, or to track you across other sites for advertising.

What this site currently uses

does not set its own analytics, advertising, or cross-site tracking cookies on this site. The only cookies that may be set are:

  • Cloudflare security cookies — our host may set a strictly necessary cookie (for example __cf_bm) to distinguish real visitors from bots and protect the site. See Cloudflare's cookie reference.
  • Third-party cookies on services you choose to use — if you click through to our scheduling provider (Calendly or similar) or submit the form (processed by Formspree), those services may set their own cookies under their own policies.

Under most cookie rules, strictly necessary cookies of this kind do not require consent. Analytics and advertising cookies do.

If you are the site owner: before you add tracking

The moment you add Google Analytics, a Meta Pixel, a TikTok Pixel, or any similar script to this site, this page must be updated and — in the EEA, the UK, and a growing number of other jurisdictions — you must obtain consent before those scripts load. That means:

  • Add a consent banner that blocks non-essential scripts until the visitor agrees
  • List each new cookie here: its name, who sets it, what it does, and how long it lasts
  • Update the "Cookies" section of the Privacy Policy to match

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Because this site does not depend on non-essential cookies, blocking them will not stop the pages, the qualifier form, or the booking flow from working.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page whenever the cookies used on this site change. The effective date above always reflects the current version.
