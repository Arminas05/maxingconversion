# The legal pages are kept out of search results by the
#   <meta name="robots" content="noindex, follow">
# tag in each one — NOT by a Disallow rule here. Those two look like they
# do the same job but conflict: a Disallow stops crawlers fetching the
# page at all, so they never read the noindex, and the bare URL can still
# end up listed. Allowing the crawl is what lets the noindex be obeyed,
# and "follow" still passes link equity back to the pages that matter.
#
# Crawl everything; the pages themselves say what may be indexed.
User-agent: *
Allow: /
